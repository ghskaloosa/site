About Our Institution

PM SHRI Govt. Girls High School Kaloosa Bandipora stands as a beacon of women's empowerment through quality education. The institution provides state-of-the-art ICT labs, digital smart classrooms, modern science laboratories, and comprehensive sports facilities under the central PM SHRI scheme.
